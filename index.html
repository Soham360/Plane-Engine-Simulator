A QUICK START

Explore with purpose.

01 / Find the bypass flow

Leave Cutaway and Airflow enabled. Blue particles travel around the core; orange particles pass through the combustor. Try a turbojet preset to remove the bypass stream.

02 / Design for a different mission

Increase fan diameter and bypass ratio for more low-speed thrust. Try a smaller, low-bypass engine at higher Mach numbers and compare fuel use.

03 / Take it to altitude

Choose Cruise conditions, then compare sea-level thrust with flight at 11,000 m. Thinner air reduces the mass entering the engine.

What does this model calculate?

A simplified separate-flow Brayton cycle with a standard-atmosphere approximation, compressor and turbine efficiencies, shaft power balance and pressure-matched nozzle estimates. It includes rotor spool-up, but excludes detailed component maps, surge, choking, afterburners and installation losses.
